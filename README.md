# Drafting the Hydrant Valve

App Store Description

Drafting the Hydrant Valve is an industrial heritage catalog and hydraulic head-loss simulator designed for municipal engineering preservationists, historic fire-apparatus collectors, and waterworks historians. This application provides a structural ledger for cataloging mid-19th-century cast-iron municipal hydrant barrels, leather flap-gate foot valves, square operating-nut drive ratios, and frost-case sleeve tolerances, capturing early municipal water supply mechanics before modern standardized fittings.

## Bespoke Database Fields

	Hydrant Barrel Registry: Unique municipal waterworks tracking ledger (e.g., DHV-HYD-512-VALVE).
  
	Operating Nut Geometry: Shape and dimension of the top valve stem nut (e.g., 1.5"-inch"  square pentagon-drive nut).
  
	Foot Valve Gate Style: Internal sealing mechanism at the main water branch (e.g., Leather-faced compression wedge, rubber-seated flap gate, reverse-flow plug).
  
	Frost Sleeve Clearance: Radial gap between the outer cast-iron protective jacket and the inner water barrel (e.g., 0.5"-inch"  air gap).
  
	Nozzle Thread Standard: Non-standardized historical hose connection threading (e.g., 3.25-inch 4-TPI V-thread municipal special).
  
	Main Valve Stem Pitch: Thread count on the internal operating rod driving the lower gate (e.g., Acme double-lead 6-TPI rod).
  
	Barrel Metal Thickness: Wall thickness of the main vertical riser casting (e.g., 0.625"-inch"  grey iron casting).
  
	Static Head Pressure Rating: Designed water column pressure limit (e.g., 85" PSI"  / 60 meters head).
  
	Municipal District Branch: Fictionalized historical urban water district, mill yard, or rail terminal (e.g., Ward 4 Water District, Iron Docks Terminal, Millside Canal Ward).
